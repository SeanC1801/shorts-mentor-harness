## Research log
- Geometry check (Claude, Python, 2026-11-02): an equatorial ring of outer radius r (in Earth radii) rises above an observer's horizon at latitude φ only if r·cos(φ) > 1. Hidden poleward of 48.2° (r=1.5), 60.0° (r=2), 66.4° (r=2.5), 70.5° (r=3). At the equator the ring lies in a plane containing the observer, so it is edge-on and passes through the zenith.
