## Draft narration
1. **[ANTI-HOOK]** A kid opens the curtains on a Tuesday morning. There's a stripe across the sky. `[C1]`
2. **[EARLY PIVOT + assumption]** It's a ring, like Saturn's, only rock. Ice wouldn't last this close to the Sun. `[C2, C3]`
3. **[RULE OF THREE — the tour]** At the equator, it's a thin line overhead. Farther north, a wide arch. Far enough north, it never clears the horizon. `[C4, C5, C6]`
4. **[VIEWER-VOICE TURN]** Now the strange part: Earth may really have had one. `[C7]`
5. **[EVIDENCE]** Around 466 million years ago, a study suggests, an asteroid passed too close, and Earth's pull tore it apart into a ring. `[C7]`
6. **[CLUE]** The clue: twenty-one craters from that era all formed near the equator. `[C8]`
7. **[HEDGED REFRAME]** Not everyone agrees. But if the study is right, Earth once wore a ring, long before the dinosaurs. `[C9, C10]`

About 114 words.
