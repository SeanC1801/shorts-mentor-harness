Append dated, episode-linked observations. Separate measured viewer response from interpretation; record evidence, confidence, and what to try next. Do not infer universal algorithm rules from one episode.

## 2026-11-11 — EP004 (script review, after scenes were in production)
- **Observation (creator, unmeasured):** the EP004 script may feel like information overload: three ideas in 53 s (how rain is made, the what-if, the cloud-sunshade twist), and the what-if promised by the title arrives at 36% after an 18 s water-cycle setup with no stakes.
- **Decision:** keep script v2 and finish production (too deep to restart).
- **Try next (hypothesis):** put the what-if premise in the first sentence, give the setup at most two sentences, and keep one main idea per Short. Do a "flow and overload" read with the creator at the idea/script gate, before any audio or rendering.
- **Evidence to check:** compare EP004's "stayed to watch" and average view duration against EP003 (65.4%, 0:41 of 0:53) once posted.

## 2026-11-13 — EP004 posted; reference channels compared
- **Measured (creator screenshots, first 13 hours):** EP004 1.1K views, 61.5% stayed, average 0:34 of 0:54 (63%). EP003: 65.4% stayed, 0:41 of 0:53 (78%).
- **Interpretation (low confidence, early data):** EP004 holds viewers less well than EP003, which fits the 2026-11-11 worry that its what-if arrives at 19 s. Check again when the retention graph is ready.
- **Measured (two reference channels, six videos each, one week old):** with one look per channel, views range 12K–919K (Jaywalk) and 50K–2.59M (Afterhour Theory). The top videos are a famous name or a big timely release, in second person, with stakes in the first sentence.
- **Interpretation:** topic and first line outweigh animation style. Their weakest still beats our best (12K vs 1.9K), so style, captions, length or timing may also matter; this data cannot separate them.
- **Found:** the "box" around our characters is the ink-line pass treating each flat character card as a solid rectangle. Details and the full comparison: `02_CONTEXT/source-analysis/reference-video-analysis-2026-11-13.md`.
- **Try next (creator direction, 2026-11-13):** 3D characters built like the references', and their colour and shading approach.
