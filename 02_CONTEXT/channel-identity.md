# Channel identity
A curious guide explores strange real-world inventions, overlooked history, and scientific possibilities. The promise: make an odd question understandable, memorable, and fun in under a minute when the story supports it. Voice: curious, clear, lively, lightly humorous; never condescending or falsely certain. Each Short is built around one central question and a visual payoff. Audience and differentiators should be refined by the creator over time; do not invent audience research.

- **Content pillars (creator, 2026-11-02; these replace the earlier B/C/D list):**
  1. **Weird Technology:** strange inventions, bizarre technological experiments, unusual AI developments, and fascinating stories about technology. Creator's example title: "The Computer That Was Powered by Water".
  2. **Strange History:** forgotten historical events, unusual decisions, strange inventions from the past, and unbelievable true stories. Creator's example title: "The Time Australia Went to War With Birds".
  3. **Scientific What-If Scenarios:** imaginative hypothetical situations explored using real scientific concepts, with funny and visually engaging explanations. Creator's example title: "What If Earth Stopped Spinning for One Second?".
