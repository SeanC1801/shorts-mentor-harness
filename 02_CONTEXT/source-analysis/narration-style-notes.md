## Observed patterns
1. **Short runtime, compact script.** About 28–37 seconds, roughly 75–95 words, in six to eight caption beats. Pace is about one sentence every four to five seconds.
2. **Opens inside a scene or striking fact.** The first sentence starts mid-action or with an odd fact and withholds who, why, and what it means. The title carries the promise, so the narration does not restate it. This is the closest match to the creator's "anti-hook."
3. **One pivot, then the explanation.** After the scene, a plain connective marks the shift into why it happened (a causal explanation). The pivot appears roughly a quarter to a third of the way in.
4. **Causal chains.** The explanation links short cause-effect steps with simple connectors, one new fact per clause, so the viewer can follow without prior knowledge.
5. **Last line lands the consequence.** Stories end on the final fact or outcome that reframes the opening, with no outro or call to action. One of the four ends on an explicit opinion; the others stay factual.
6. **Plain, conversational language.** Short common words, steady and calm delivery. Emotion comes from the content, not from heightened phrasing or exclamations.
7. **Honest hedging.** Qualifiers such as "according to the story" and "people thought" mark uncertain claims instead of stating them as fact.
8. **Viewer placement varies.** Most are third-person; one uses second person to put the viewer in the scene.

## Use in this channel
- Open on a concrete scene or fact with the explanation withheld; let the title or image supply the promise.
- Use one clear pivot into the mechanism, then a cause-effect chain, then end on the consequence.
- Keep sentences plain and about one idea each; hedge uncertain claims in the wording itself.
- Aim for a compact, evidence-limited script, with length set by the story and measured narration, not a quota.

## Boundaries
- Do not reuse his topics, titles, sentences, or connective habits as a signature. Keep the channel voice from `channel-identity.md`.
- Many of his topics are dark; our channel's tone is curious and lightly humorous. Adopt structure, not subject matter or tone.
- Do not claim these patterns cause views or retention.
