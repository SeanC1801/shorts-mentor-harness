# Character style notes: stick-limb cartoon references (craft only)

**Source:** two sets of screenshots supplied by the creator on 2026-11-10.
- **Set A:** three vertical Short frames with word-by-word captions (an office worker at an elevator panel, a pirate on a ship deck, a police officer in a kitchen). The channel was not named.
- **Set B:** six long-form thumbnails from TheOdd1sOut's channel ("Annoying Customers", "Tabletop Games", "Wrong Numbers" and others; 100M+ views each).

**Evidence limits:** still images only. Motion, timing and narration were not seen. View counts say nothing about why the videos work. Craft only: **never copy a character, face, costume, logo, thumbnail or lettering**. FooFilms characters must be original and must not be mistaken for either channel's.

## What the references share
1. **The head is the character.** The head takes 35–50% of the height and holds nearly all the acting. Bodies are minimal.
2. **No realistic anatomy.** There are no noses or ears (or only a hint), no fingers (rounded nubs), and no necks.
3. **Thick, even outlines** in a dark colour, slightly hand-drawn. They read at phone size.
4. **Flat fills** with little or no shading on the character, set against a richer background.
5. **Identity through one costume piece.** A hat, tie, uniform or hair colour tells you the role before anyone speaks.
6. **Expression does the comedy.** The eyebrows and eyelids carry the emotion.

## Set A specifics
- **Shape:** a large white round head on a small capsule torso, with very thin stick arms and long stick legs drawn as single dark strokes.
- **Face:** small dark oval eyes, heavy flat eyebrows, and a default **half-lidded, deadpan or smug** look. The mouth is a short curve.
- **Line:** a dark brown-black outline of even weight.
- **Costume carries the role:** a tie and pocket for the office, a tricorn hat and sash for the pirate, a cap with a star and a badge for the police.
- **Staging:** the characters stand in front of **richly painted, detailed backgrounds** with depth of field and warm light. The flat character against the rich set is the main contrast.
- **Captions:** bold white words with a black outline, one or two at a time, in the lower middle of the frame.

## Set B specifics
- **Shape:** a white bean body where the head and body merge into one shape, with short stubby limbs.
- **Face:** very large round eyes, and **huge open mouths with a red inside** at the peak of an emotion (anger, panic).
- **Supporting cast:** they get hair, glasses and clothing colours, and the bodies stay the same.
- **Thumbnails:** a big hand-lettered title, one or two faces at peak expression, and a simple background.

## Lessons for FooFilms (hypotheses)
- Make the **head big and the body simple**, and let the eyebrows act.
- Give each character **one signature costume piece** that also fits the episode's topic.
- Use **thick, dark, even outlines with flat fills plus at most one cel shade band**, so the character matches the cel look of EP003 onward.
- Keep the **flat character against a rich set**. Our Blender cel sets already do this.
- Save **big-mouth peak expressions** for the turns of the story, like the trigger and the reversal.

## Do not copy
- Set A's exact combination of a plain white circle head, dot eyes and a smug half-lid on a capsule body with stick legs.
- Set B's merged white bean body with giant eyes; it is the channel's signature.
- Any costume, prop, logo, thumbnail layout or hand-lettering from either set.
