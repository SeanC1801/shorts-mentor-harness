# Production standards

- Story: one central question; curious, entertaining, slightly humorous English narration; build a clear promise, escalation/emotional turn, visual explanation, and satisfying payoff. Duration follows story and narration, not a forced quota.
- Originality: creator owns the premise and creative approvals. Use transcript research only for attributed general lessons; do not imitate a person's voice, signature style, or wording.
- Research: cite claims with source title, publisher/author, URL, access date, and the claim supported. Distinguish fact, interpretation, uncertainty, and hypothetical. Do not present algorithm, earnings, or performance claims as established without independent evidence.
- Visuals: original expressive 3D characters in dense, dressed sets with flat colour fills, ink outlines and mood lighting (`02_CONTEXT/visual-style.md` v4); legible silhouettes and a clear focal point in the vertical frame. Maintain asset IDs and provenance.
- Delivery target: 1080x1920, 30 fps master, rendered in Blender (3D sets, 3D characters). Characters and camera update every frame. Individual scene MP4s and review preview for CapCut. Look and motion follow `02_CONTEXT/visual-style.md` (v4) and `motion-rules.md` (v3). EP002–EP004 were made under earlier versions.
- Rendering: ask the creator before any render; one scene at a time, each approved before the next; launch through the scene launcher, never a hand-typed shell loop (`01_SPECS/render-pipeline-notes.md`).
- Testing: a change to format, look or workflow is a test until its results are in (`03_TRACKING/format-tests.md`).
- Audio: ElevenLabs generated narration is the measured timing source. Keep narration, ambience, SFX and music on separate identified tracks; document ranges and ducking. Respect rights and retain provenance.
- Export and publish: human reviews and edits in CapCut. No automated posting or publication.
