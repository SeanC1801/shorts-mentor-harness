# Creator preferences
- The creator supplies the original seed or explicitly selects a direction. AI may clarify, research, structure, and recommend; it does not claim authorship of the premise.
- Topics: Scientific What-If scenarios are the main niche (2026-11-10), kept broad; Strange History is the second pillar; Weird Technology was discarded (2026-11-10). One central question per Short. Topics are picked in the topic stage of `/short-ideate`.
- Hook style: the default is an **anti-hook**: open understated, concrete, and context-light to maximize curiosity, then build the full context gradually through to the payoff. The promise must stay honest; curiosity is never bait. **Under test (2026-11-13):** a stakes-first opening that states the premise and stakes in the first sentence. Each concept card says which opening the episode uses (`03_TRACKING/format-tests.md`).
- Narration craft: take structural inspiration from the Zack D. Films transcripts (see `02_CONTEXT/source-analysis/narration-style-notes.md`): scene-first anti-hook, one pivot, a cause-effect chain, a final-consequence ending, plain language, honest hedging. Do not copy any creator's voice or wording. Also draw on the Ricks Clips transcripts (`narration-style-notes-ricks-clips.md`): an absurd concrete first sentence, an early pivot, rule-of-three rhythm, a reframing final reveal, dry humor. Dramatized dialogue must be labeled illustrative. No MrBeast transcript has been analyzed.
- English stories that feel curious, entertaining, and slightly humorous. Target length is flexible; use the idea and evidence to determine duration.
- **Test before finalizing (2026-11-13):** every change to how videos are made is tested on real episodes before it becomes a rule. The register is `03_TRACKING/format-tests.md`.
- Animation inspiration: Ricks Clips, Jaywalk and Afterhour Theory (see `02_CONTEXT/source-analysis/reference-video-analysis-2026-11-13.md`, and the earlier `animation-style-notes-ricks-clips.md` and `animation-style-notes-3d-cartoon-reference.md`): simple expressive 3D characters in rich, dressed sets; light and glow for mood; in-scene props and signage; large word-by-word captions. Craft inspiration only; characters and designs must be original.
- Look (visual style v4, approved 2026-11-13, from EP005): flat-colour 3D with original 3D characters (bean build, painted swappable faces), dense dressed sets and mood lighting. Avoid imitation of transcript creators or recognizable characters. EP002–EP004 used earlier looks and stay as posted.
- Stack (from 2026-11-02): Claude Code + Blender 3D (EEVEE). 1080x1920, 30 fps master; characters and camera update every frame, camera always moving (motion rules v3). EP002 was made with Remotion at 24 fps. Individual scene MP4s plus preview for CapCut editing.
- Production rules: ask before any render, stills or full; scenes are approved one by one; the creator runs git; every approved preview comes with title and hashtag suggestions; keep the Mac plugged in with the lid open during renders.
- ElevenLabs narration is timing authority once generated. Sync shots to actual narration; do not distort speech to fit a target duration.
- The creator edits in CapCut. No auto-publication. All approvals are explicit, version-bound, and recorded.
